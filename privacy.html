Appalaxy · Mann Mandir

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

In short
  • Mann Mandir has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your settings, chosen deity, reminder times and puja days are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device and usage information to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services, and we do not sell your information.

1. Who we are

Mann Mandir (the “app”, package com.appalaxy.mannmandir) is published by Appalaxy (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is involved when you use the app on Android and the choices you have. If you have any question, write to support@appalaxy.com.

2. Information stored only on your phone

To remember your preferences, the app saves the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • your settings: language, music, vibration and visual quality;
  • the deity you have chosen;
  • whether reminders are on, and their morning and evening times;
  • the days on which you completed a puja (used for your streak and the My Darshan Days calendar), and whether today’s diya is lit.

This information never leaves your phone and is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android’s settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google. Ads appear as a banner on some screens and as a full-screen ad after a completed puja. To show, limit, measure and protect these ads against fraud, Google’s software in the app collects and sends to Google information such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived;
  • device and app information, such as the phone model, Android version, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped), and diagnostic information about the ad software.

Google handles this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

We ask Google to show only ads suitable for general and parental-guidance audiences (ratings G and PG).

Your advertising choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google’s consent form before any ads are requested, so you can decide whether your information may be used for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → About → Privacy choices. If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads.
  • Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the phone).

4. Reminders

If you turn on aarti reminders, the app schedules them on your phone with Android’s notification system. No server is involved and we do not send push messages. You can turn reminders off in the app’s Settings, or block the app’s notifications in Android’s settings.

5. Sharing blessings

When you tap Share Blessings, the app creates a blessing image and opens Android’s share menu. You choose the app or person to share with. We do not receive the image or know where it was shared.

6. Device features

The app uses some features of your phone only on the phone itself, and does not store or send what they report:

  • the motion sensor, for the gentle tilt effect in the temple;
  • vibration, for touch feedback (you can turn it off in Settings);
  • the amount of memory in your phone, to choose a suitable visual quality;
  • your phone’s language, to show the app in English or Hindi until you choose one.

The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not create accounts or ask you to sign in.
  • We do not run our own servers that collect information from the app.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.

8. Children

Mann Mandir is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and that information about them has been collected through the ads, please contact us and we will help you contact Google about it.

9. Keeping information and your rights

We do not hold personal information about you: what the app stores stays on your phone (section 2), and you can delete it by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. Google keeps the advertising information it collects for the periods described in its own policies.

Depending on where you live, data protection laws, such as India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, and the laws of some US states, may give you rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold such information, most requests about advertising data are best made to Google, using the controls above or through Google’s privacy pages. You are always welcome to contact us at support@appalaxy.com and we will help where we can.

10. Security

Information saved by the app stays in its private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Communication between Google’s ad software and Google is encrypted by Google.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app gains new features. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change affects how information is handled, we will update this page before the change reaches the app.
